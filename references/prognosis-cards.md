# Per-lamp outlook cards

Defaults for SKILL Step 6. Read with `references/prognosis.md`. Do not diagnose. Do not invent pounds.

Each card is: bucket, repair call, sell talk, close-it-yourself, red lines.

---

## 1 `oil-pressure`

**Bucket:** `poor`

**Repair:** No published engine-rebuild or oil-pump job. Say we publish no figure for this class of work. Two written estimates only if they already recovered the car and a garage is quoting.

**Sell:** Get a bid **as it sits** (usually not a runner). Recovery is part of the cost. Compare any engine estimate with that bid before authorising a rebuild.

**Close it yourself:** No. A cold dipstick reading does not make it safe to restart and does not close the fault. No scan-tool fix.

**Red lines:** Do not say it is the pump, the sender, or a bearing. Do not give DIY oil-pressure-switch steps.

---

## 2 `coolant-temp` (red)

**Bucket:** `poor`

**Repair:** You may call `head-gasket-repair-cost`. It is often `gbp: null` — then say no figure. Do not say the gasket has failed. A caught-early thermostat job has no published slug: still no invented pounds.

**Sell:** If they kept driving, the estimate can exceed what the car will fetch. Bid as it sits vs written estimate.

**Close it yourself:** No. Once cold, looking at the tank and the floor is information for the garage, not a repair. Never open a hot cap.

**Red lines:** No “it’s the head gasket.” No flushing tutorial.

---

## 3 `brake-system`

**Parking brake still on, pedal normal:** skip outlook. Not a fault.

**Lamp stays on with parking brake / EPB / Auto Hold off, or spongy pedal / pull / leak:** `poor`

**Repair:** Do not call pads-and-discs as the cause of a hydraulic Stop lamp. If they already said the garage quoted pads and discs, you may call `brake-pads-and-discs-cost` as that **invoice class**. Otherwise: no published figure for hydraulic / ABS-combined brake-lamp work.

**Sell:** A car that must not be driven is sold with collection. Bid vs estimate.

**Close it yourself:** No bleeding, no pads, no lifting.

---

## 4 `airbag-srs`

**Bucket:** `garage` (controllable on many cars; still no DIY). Upgrade to `poor` only if they already have a large SRS quote or the car is otherwise not worth a safety repair.

**Repair:** No published SRS slug. No figure.

**Sell:** Only if they are choosing not to repair a safety system — say that a lamp-on airbag car is worth less and may not be legal to sell as a safe runner. Still no invented bid.

**Close it yourself:** No. No scan-clear as a fix. No clock-spring DIY.

---

## 5 `power-steering`

**Bucket:** `garage`. `poor` if steering is already heavy and they cannot safely get there (that is Stop in drive advice).

**Repair:** No published PAS slug. No figure. Two estimates.

**Sell:** Only on the Stop / cannot-steer case: bid as it sits vs estimate.

**Close it yourself:** No fluid-for-life guessing. A reservoir glance if the handbook shows one is information, not a close.

---

## 6 `engine-steady`

**Bucket:** `device` then `garage` if the lamp stays after a read (or they have no reader).

**Repair:** Always call `car-diagnostic-test-cost` for the garage path. Do **not** call cat, clutch, or belt slugs because this lamp is on.

**Sell:** Not first-line. If a later garage estimate is large, switch speech to weak outlook then.

**Close it yourself:** Fuel cap clicked. Then a small reader: stored code **and** freeze frame, written down, **not cleared**. Link scanner guide / category below drive advice. Off-the-shelf SKUs: do not invent stock.

**Red lines:** Not “it’s the lambda / cat / coil.”

---

## 7 `engine-flashing`

**Bucket:** `poor`

**Repair:** `car-diagnostic-test-cost`. You may add `catalytic-converter-replacement-cost` only as “if the garage later invoices a converter” — not as today’s diagnosis. Aftertreatment damage is why outlook is weak, not a named part.

**Sell:** Recovery + bid as it sits vs any large exhaust / engine estimate.

**Close it yourself:** No. Do not keep driving to “see.” No DIY coils.

---

## 8 `battery-charging`

**Bucket:** `garage`. ICE + heavy steering / rising temp / belt noise: `poor` (Stop already in drive advice). EV: stay `garage`, no belt combo.

**Repair:** You may call `car-battery-replacement-cost` as “if they invoice a 12V battery” and `alternator-replacement-cost` (often null) as “if they invoice charging repair.” Do not choose which it is.

**Sell:** On the ICE Stop combo: bid vs estimate; the car may not remain a runner.

**Close it yourself:** No. Switching off heaters is drive advice, not a repair.

---

## 9 `dpf` (diesel board only)

**Steady, driving normally:** `owner` then `garage` if one handbook regen does not clear it.

**Flash / limp / oil over maximum:** `poor`

**Repair:** Call `dpf-cleaning-cost` (often null). There is no published DPF-replace slug — do not invent a filter price.

**Sell:** On the poor branch: a missing or failed factory DPF is a test and value problem. Bid vs estimate. Do not suggest deletion as a cheap fix.

**Close it yourself:** One handbook regen if the car is driving normally. Oil level check. Never enclosed-space regen. Never scan-tool forced regen. Never “just cut it out.”

---

## 10 `tyre-pressure`

**Simply on:** `owner`

**Flash ~1 minute at startup, then steady:** `garage` after they have still checked pressures

**Repair:** No TPMS slug. If a tyre is destroyed, that is a tyre bill — we publish no figure; do not invent.

**Sell:** No.

**Close it yourself:** Cold pressures to the **door-sill or flap placard**, not the tyre sidewall. Nail / bulge check. Handbook reset. If pressures are correct and the malfunction pattern remains, garage — not a reader “clear.”

---

## 11 `abs`

**Bucket:** `garage`

**Repair:** No ABS slug. No figure. Do not call wheel-bearing unless they already named that job.

**Sell:** No, unless they already have a large hydraulic quote.

**Close it yourself:** No. Normal brakes still work; that is drive advice, not a DIY ABS bleed.

---

## 12 `esc-traction`

**Flashing while driving:** skip. Not a fault.

**Steady, button was off:** skip once they turn it back on and the lamp clears.

**Steady, not switched off:** `garage`

**Repair:** No slug. No figure.

**Sell:** No.

**Close it yourself:** Traction button only. Then garage. Not a remap, not a sensor DIY.

---

## 13 `glow-plug`

**On with ignition, then out:** skip. Not a fault. No outlook.

**Stays on or flashes while running:** `garage`

**Repair:** `car-diagnostic-test-cost`. No glow-plug slug. Do not invent a set-of-four price.

**Sell:** No unless a later estimate is large.

**Close it yourself:** No glow-plug replacement DIY.

---

## Unmatched petrol / hybrid GPF

Not lamp 9. Not diesel regen.

**Bucket:** `device` then `garage`

**Repair:** `car-diagnostic-test-cost` only.

**Close it yourself:** Scan + freeze frame, do not clear. No motorway regen copy.

---

## Unmatched AdBlue / urea / DEF

Not 9, not 6.

**Low level, car still starts normally:** `owner` — correct fluid, handbook filler.

**Remaining-starts / will not start:** `poor` — recovery or collection; no published AdBlue-system slug.

**Close it yourself:** Top-up only on the low-level branch. No tank-reset DIY. No “just add water.”

---

## Unmatched EV (turtle / car-with-! no skids / HV or charge-plug message)

Not 12, not 8, not an ICE unknown board.

**Bucket:** `garage` (or `poor` if they already have a traction-pack quote)

**Repair:** No ICE slugs. No invented HV battery price.

**Sell:** If a pack quote exists, compare it with a bid as it sits. Still do not invent the bid.

**Close it yourself:** No.

---

## Electric board lamp 8 (12V rectangle)

**Bucket:** `garage`

**Repair:** `car-battery-replacement-cost` as “if invoiced.” No alternator / belt story.

**Close it yourself:** No.
