# Weak outlook without a sell number

A missing pound is not a missing outlook.

On weak outlook only: we publish no used-car price. Get one bid as it sits and one written garage estimate. Put the estimate next to the bid before they authorise the job. If the estimate is larger than the bid, selling is often the better outlook. Do not invent pounds.

Pass versus fail:

- Pass: get one bid as it sits; compare it with the estimate.
- Fail: skip the outlook because pounds are missing, or invent a used-car price.
- Pass: the repair may cost more than a buyer will pay as it sits.
- Fail: push a sale on garage or close-it-yourself.
