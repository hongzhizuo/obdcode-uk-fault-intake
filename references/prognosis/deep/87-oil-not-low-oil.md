# 87 · Oil-pressure is not “add a litre”

Not a second SKILL. If this note disagrees with `SKILL.md`, `references/prognosis.md`, `references/prognosis-cards.md`, or `references/value-gain.md`, those four win. Live card: lamp 1 `oil-pressure` in `prognosis-cards.md`. Do not invent pounds. Do not diagnose. Do not add a 14th lamp id.

The red oil-can is the most misunderstood UK dashboard lamp. It does **not** mean the oil is low. It means the oil is not being **pumped at pressure**. That is an unpressurised engine, not a top-up reminder.

**Id:** `oil-pressure` (circled **1**)  
**Bucket:** `poor` (always on this lamp — not because the car is old)  
**Owner hears:** Repair may cost more than the car  
**Drive advice:** Stop. Switch off. Do not restart. Recovery or collection.  
**Close-it-yourself:** No. A cold dipstick is information. Top-up is not a close.

---

## When

Run this misconception line in the **same turn** as the Stop statement and the `poor` outlook whenever the showing lamp is the **red oil-can** (named, or circled 1 on a petrol / diesel / hybrid / unknown ICE board).

Skip when:

- Key-on **bulb check** that then went out — not a fault; no outlook.
- **Electric** board — slot 1 is not printed. Do not map a turtle, HV text, or lamp 8 to this card.
- Value-only wrap / service talk with **no** lamp — that is `value-gain.md`.

If they already poured oil in, or the dipstick later reads full: **still this card.** Level does not rewrite the lamp. Do not upgrade to Close it yourself.

Vans: same bucket. Say **your Transit**.

---

## What the lamp is (and is not)

| It is | It is not |
|---|---|
| Oil not at **pressure** | “The sump is a litre low” |
| Unpressurised bearings; damage can start in well under a minute | A reminder to buy oil at a supermarket |
| Stop, switch off, do not restart | “Top up and it’ll go out” / “creep to the garage” |
| `poor` outlook; usually not a runner | A driveway close |

A correct cold level does **not** clear the meaning of the lamp and does **not** make the car safe to restart. A low level, if they find one once the engine is completely cold, is still only information for the garage. Pouring a litre in does not restore pressure and is not a repair.

Do **not** then say: so it is the pump; so it is the sender; so it is a bearing; so it is the switch. Full or low, the lamp does not name the part.

Hot oil and hot engine parts cause serious burns (`W-HOT`). Do not pull the dipstick, filler cap, or any sender while the engine is hot.

## Owner copy

≤120 words. Spoken **[Outlook]** after the statement. Stop / recovery already sits in **[Drive advice]** — no shop or scanner link above it.

The red oil-can does not mean add a litre. The oil is not at pressure. An unpressurised engine can be wrecked in well under a minute. Stop, switch off, and do not restart to get home or to a garage. Arrange recovery. Repair may cost more than the car. We publish no figure for this class of work. Get one bid as it sits — it is not a runner — and one written estimate. Recovery is part of the sell cost. Once the engine is completely cold, a dipstick reading is information for the garage, not a close. A full reading does not make it safe to restart. Topping up does not close this. The lamp does not name the part.

## Close-it-yourself

**No.** Outlook is `poor`. Red-class. Close-it-yourself needs a **good** outlook **and** an owner-safe or device step. Neither applies.

- A reader does **not** fix oil pressure. No scan-tool close. Do not clear codes.
- Cold dipstick (engine **completely** cool): information only. Not permission to restart.
- **Top-up is not a close.** Do not add a litre and drive. Do not “just pop it to the garage.”
- Do not give oil-pressure-switch, sender, or pressure-test steps.

Owner facts (how long the lamp was on, any noise, last oil change) go in **[Since]** or **[Ask the garage]** — process, not a parts shortlist.

## Red lines

1. Do not say it is the **pump**, the **sender**, or a **bearing**. Do not pick pump vs sender from the dipstick.
2. Do not treat this lamp as low oil. Do not teach “add a litre.”
3. Do not diagnose. The lamp does not name the part.
4. No DIY switch, sender, or pressure-test steps.
5. No invented pounds for repair, sell, recovery, or “typical rebuild.”
6. Never advise clearing the lamp. Never treat a cold dipstick or a top-up as safe to restart.
7. Safety before commerce. No scanner, shop, or parts link above Stop / recovery.
8. No SAE J2012 wording. No real registration. Illustrative only: `AB12CDE`, then **your 2016 Fiesta** / **your Transit**.
9. Electric: do not invent this lamp. MOT: no automatic fail from this lamp; an engine that cannot safely run cannot complete a test — do not say “Expect a fail.”
