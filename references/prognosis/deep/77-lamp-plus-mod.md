# Lamp plus wrap / service value

Deep note only. Live rules win: `SKILL.md`, `references/prognosis.md`, `references/prognosis-cards.md`, `references/value-gain.md`.

Owner has a **lamp** and also asks about wrap, service, or sale-price effect. That is **both** paths: Path A then a separate **[Value]** block. Safety before cosmetics. A wrap does not fix a lamp.

## When

Run this combined order when **both** are true in the same chat (often the same message):

1. A lamp is lit, or they named a warning light / garage card — Path A (SKILL Steps 1–6).
2. They also name wrap, wheels, paint, PPF, service, cambelt, clutch, tyres, vehicle card, or “does this add value?”

**When not:** wrap / service / value and **no** lamp → Path B only (`value-gain.md`, example N). No picker. No outlook. Do not use this file to bolt a fault statement onto a cosmetics question.

If Step 5 already ended as **not-a-fault** (glow 13 went out, blue thermometer that went out, parking-brake brake lamp, ESC flashing while driving): skip Step 6, then still a separate **[Value]** for the work they named.

If the wrap / value question arrives **after** the statement: restate **[Drive advice]** and **[Outlook]**, then **[Value]**. Do not switch to Path B only.

## Order

1. Spoken fault statement (~60–80 words), including **[Drive advice]**.
2. Spoken **[Outlook]** (~40–60 words) from the **lamp** card. Wrap and service history do not change the bucket.
3. Device or shop links **below** Stop / recovery. Never above a Stop line.
4. Separate **[Value]** then **[Record]** for the named work (`value-gain.md`).
5. Stop. Do not diagnose. Do not teach the wrap.

Do not merge wrap into **[Outlook]** as a close. Do not put **[Value]** above Stop.

Lamp repair slugs stay on that lamp’s allowlist. A cambelt they asked about as **value** may use `cambelt-and-water-pump-cost` (or clutch / MOT slugs) for the **job** range inside **[Value]** — never as the cause of an engine lamp. Gain stays a band. No invented uplift pounds.

## Owner copy

≤120 words. Fictional 2016 Fiesta only. Labels are for the agent; the owner hears the sentences.

**Oil lamp + wrap (Stop first):**

Your 2016 Fiesta is showing a red oil-can. Stop. Do not drive it in. Ask the garage to collect, or call recovery. Repair may cost more than the car. We publish no engine-rebuild figure. Get a bid as it sits and a written estimate. Recovery is part of the sell cost. A wrap does not fix this lamp and does not make it safe to drive. Buyers treat a wrap as taste — Little / mixed, rarely pound-for-pound. Service history usually moves the bid more. Record any later invoice on your vehicle card at obdcode.co.uk.

**Steady engine lamp + service (scan first):**

Your 2016 Fiesta is showing a steady amber engine outline. Drive with care. If it starts flashing, do not drive it in. A scan is the next step; the lamp does not name the part. Do not clear it. A service stamp does not turn the lamp off. Buyers often pay more for documented service history — Strong — than for a wrap. That is sale-price effect, not a repair. Repeat your invoice as what you paid, not as uplift. Record date, mileage, and the invoice on your vehicle card at obdcode.co.uk.

## Pass versus fail

**Pass**

- Statement, then outlook, then a **separate** **[Value]** block.
- Stop / drive advice before any wrap or “adds value” line.
- “A wrap does not fix this lamp.”
- Wrap / wheels: **Little / mixed**. Documented service / cambelt / clutch invoice: **Strong**. Tyres / MOT: **Modest**.
- Outlook bucket still follows the lamp card (`poor` stays `poor`).
- Invoice they paid, repeated as spend. Band for gain. Vehicle-card record line.
- How-to refused. Plate not printed.

**Fail**

- Lead with wrap or value because they asked about it first.
- Skip Path A / the picture / **[Drive advice]** because they also mentioned a wrap.
- Path B only while a lamp is still lit.
- “Wrap it and sell” / “the wrap will hide the lamp.”
- Fold cosmetics into **[Outlook]** as a close-it-yourself step.
- “A wrap adds £800” or any invented gain / used-car price.
- Teach how to wrap, remap, or delete a filter.
- Call `clutch-replacement-cost` or `cambelt-and-water-pump-cost` **because** an engine lamp is on.
- Treat a Strong service band as a diagnosis or as a lamp fix.
- Place wrap / shop / scanner speech above a Stop line.
