# Legal like-for-like exhaust (Little)

Not a second SKILL. Live rules: `SKILL.md` Step 7, `references/value-gain.md`. If this file disagrees, those win. Sale-price effect only. No how-to.

A **legal like-for-like** exhaust is **Little**. Quiet quality can help; a **roar usually hurts**. **Illegal noise / off-map** exhaust is **Negative** — not this card (`71-illegal-mods-negative.md`). Do not instruct.

## When

- Path B: no lamp, and they name a legal like-for-like exhaust, a replacement silencer, or “I’ve just had an exhaust done,” or they ask whether that work **adds value**.
- Lookup the plate if they gave one, for year / make / model in the value line. **No** dashboard picker.
- Lamp also lit: fault statement + repair-or-sell outlook **first**, then this **[Value]** block. An exhaust does not close a lamp.
- They describe a roar, drone, or “loud pipe” that they still present as a legal swap: stay on this card. Band remains **Little**. Say the roar usually puts buyers off.
- They name illegal noise, off-map, unapproved, or a delete: **Negative**. Switch to `71-illegal-mods-negative.md`. Do not teach fitting, cutting, welding, or “putting it back.”

Not this file: wrap / wheels (Little / mixed), stereo (Little), documented cambelt / service (Strong), DPF / GPF / cat delete or unapproved remap (Negative).

## Owner copy

≤120 words. Price effect only. Speak this, then stop.

```
[Value] Little. A legal like-for-like exhaust is not a premium. Quiet quality can help a careful buyer; a roar usually hurts — it shrinks the pool more than it lifts the bid. We publish no gain in pounds. The invoice you paid is spend, not uplift. A documented cambelt or service history usually moves the bid more.

[Record] Date, mileage, and that invoice on a vehicle card at obdcode.co.uk. Legal modification only.
```

If they already named a written invoice, you may repeat **that** figure once as what they paid, then this band. Paying £800 does not raise the sale price by £800.

If the work is illegal noise or off-map, do **not** speak this Little copy. Speak **[Value] Negative** from the illegal-mods card. Do not present that work as a selling point. No **[Record]** as a premium.

## Band

**Little** for a legal like-for-like exhaust. Typical UK private-sale / part-exchange reaction, not a valuation.

| Work | Band | Skill line |
|---|---|---|
| Legal like-for-like exhaust (quiet, type-approved) | Little | Quiet quality can help; a roar usually does not |
| Legal swap that still roars | Little | The roar usually hurts. Taste. Smaller pool. Not Modest, not Strong |
| Illegal noise / off-map exhaust | Negative | MOT and neighbours. Not a value-add. Other card. Do not instruct |

Do not upgrade a quiet legal pipe to Modest or Strong. Do not dump a legal like-for-like into Negative. Do not dump illegal noise into Little.

`repair_cost` has no exhaust-gain slug. Do not call a published job to invent an uplift. Gain stays a band.

## Record line

Point them to a vehicle card on [obdcode.co.uk](https://obdcode.co.uk). The skill does not build the card.

Record only **legal** work:

- What was done (legal like-for-like exhaust — plain English)
- Date and mileage
- Invoice total **they paid** (their number, repeated as spend)
- This band: **Little**

Do not print the plate. Do not invent a resale premium. Do not record illegal noise as a feature.

## Red lines

1. No how-to: no fit, cut, weld, remap, off-map, delete, or “make it quieter / louder” steps. Price effect only.
2. No invented gain pounds. Never “adds £800” or “buyers pay 40% of the invoice.” There is no modification-gain API.
3. Their invoice is **spend**, not uplift.
4. Illegal noise is **Negative**. Do not instruct. Not legal advice beyond “do not do this to a road car.” No invented fines.
5. No diagnosis of a lamp via this band. No picker on a value-only question.
6. No real plate in speech or in git.
7. Safety / legality before any “this might sound nicer to a buyer.” Do not put this block above a Stop line.
8. Do not call `repair_cost` for an exhaust as **gain**. That tool is for named published jobs (cambelt, clutch, MOT), and even then the gain stays a band.

## Pass / fail

- Pass: **[Value] Little.** Quiet quality can help; a roar usually hurts. Not a pound-for-pound gain.
- Fail: “a sports exhaust adds £800” / “buyers pay extra for a loud pipe.”
- Pass: invoice they paid, if they give one, spoken as **spend**, then Little.
- Fail: treating that invoice as sale-price uplift.
- Pass: roar on a legal like-for-like stays **Little**, with the roar as a minus for most buyers.
- Fail: calling a roar Modest or Strong, or calling a legal quiet pipe Negative.
- Pass: illegal noise / off-map → **Negative**, no how-to, other card.
- Fail: dumping illegal noise into Little; wrap / exhaust / weld how-to; “just fit a louder box.”
- Pass: “we publish no gain in pounds.” Documented cambelt or service history usually moves the bid more.
- Fail: invented uplift; opening the cluster when there is no lamp; printing the plate.
- Pass: lamp + this question → statement and outlook, then a separate **[Value]**.
- Fail: value line above Stop / recovery; diagnosing the lamp from the band; recording illegal noise as a premium on the vehicle card.
