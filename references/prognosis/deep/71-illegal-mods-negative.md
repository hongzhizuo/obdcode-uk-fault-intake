# Illegal or MOT-hostile work (Negative)

Not a second SKILL. Live rules: `SKILL.md`, `references/value-gain.md`. This file only fills **Step 7** sale-price speech for DPF / GPF / cat delete, unapproved remap / emissions cheat, and illegal / off-map exhaust. No how-to.

Legal like-for-like exhaust is **Little**, not this card. Over-modded show cars are a smaller specialist pool; still not an upgrade for most buyers.

## When

- Path B: no lamp, and they name a delete, decat, unapproved remap, emissions cheat, illegal noise, or off-map exhaust — or ask whether that work **adds value**.
- Direct ask: “will deleting the DPF raise value?”
- Lookup the plate if they gave one, for year / make / model in the value line. **No** dashboard picker.
- Lamp also lit: fault statement + repair-or-sell outlook **first**, then this **[Value]** block. A delete does not fix a lamp. Do not suggest cutting a filter as a cheap DPF / cat repair (`references/prognosis-cards.md`).

## Owner copy

≤120 words. Price effect only.

```
[Value] Negative. Removing a DPF, GPF, or catalytic converter, fitting an unapproved remap, or fitting an illegal exhaust does not raise the sale price. It is not an upgrade on a used-on-road car. Buyers, testers, and insurers treat it as a defect. The buyer pool and the bid shrink. The car can fail the MOT. We publish no gain in pounds. Do not do this to a road car.

If that work is already on the car, the band is still Negative. Documented service or a cambelt invoice is what usually moves a bid.
```

No **[Record]** as a selling point. The vehicle card is for service, repair, presentation, and **legal** modification. Do not present this work as a premium.

## Band

**Negative.** Shrinks the buyer pool or the bid. Illegal work is also a legal / test / insurer problem, not a valuation uplift.

| Work | Band | Skill line |
|---|---|---|
| DPF / GPF / cat removal | Negative | Illegal on a used-on-road car. Buyers, testers, and insurers treat it as a defect, not an upgrade |
| Unapproved remap / emissions cheat | Negative | Shrinks the pool; can fail the test; do not instruct |
| Illegal noise / off-map exhaust | Negative | MOT and neighbours. Not a value-add |

If they already have a written invoice, you may repeat **that** figure as what they paid, then this band. Spend is not uplift.

## Red lines

1. No how-to: no delete, blank, weld, remap, off-map, or “put it back” steps. Garage restoration is not this card.
2. No invented gain pounds. No “adds £800.” `repair_cost` is never a **gain**.
3. Do not call it an upgrade. Do not instruct.
4. Not legal advice beyond “do not do this to a road car.” No invented fines.
5. No diagnosis of a lamp via this band. No picker on a value-only question.
6. No real plate in speech or in git.
7. Safety / legality before any buyer-taste talk. Do not put this block above a Stop line.
8. MOT: testers may treat a missing factory filter or an illegal exhaust as a defect. Do not copy lamp speech “Expect a fail.”

## Pass / fail

- Pass: **[Value] Negative.** Not an upgrade. Buyers, testers, and insurers treat it as a defect. Do not instruct.
- Fail: “it adds power so it is worth more” / “buyers pay extra for a delete.”
- Pass: “we publish no gain in pounds.”
- Fail: invented uplift, or treating their invoice as sale-price rise.
- Pass: no cluster picture on a value-only question.
- Fail: wrap / remap / delete how-to, or “just cut it out” as a DPF fix.
- Pass: lamp + this question → statement and outlook, then a separate **[Value]**.
- Fail: value line above Stop / recovery; diagnosing the lamp from the band; printing the plate.
- Pass: quiet type-approved like-for-like exhaust stays **Little** on its own card.
- Fail: dumping a legal exhaust into Negative, or an illegal exhaust into Little / Modest.
