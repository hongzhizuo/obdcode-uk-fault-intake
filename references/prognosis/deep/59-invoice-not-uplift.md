# Invoice is spend, not sale-price uplift

Not a second SKILL. Live rules: `SKILL.md`, `references/value-gain.md`. This note only fills how **pounds** may appear in Step 7 speech. If it disagrees with those two, they win. Do not invent pounds. Do not diagnose. Do not add a 14th lamp id.

There is **no** modification-gain API. The **band** is the gain statement. An invoice they already paid may be repeated as **spend**. It is never a sale-price rise.

## When

Any Step 7 **[Value]** line — wrap, wheels, exhaust, remap, paint, PPF, stereo, racking, towbar, service, cambelt, clutch, tyres, MOT — when they:

- name a written invoice, or ask “will I get the £X back?”
- ask us to put a pound figure on what buyers will pay extra
- ask whether paying £2,000 (or any invoice) means the asking price should rise by that amount

Lookup the plate if they gave one, for year / make / model. **No** dashboard picker on a value-only question. Lamp also lit: fault statement + outlook **first**, then this money rule inside a separate **[Value]** block.

## Rule

1. **Their invoice.** If they already have a written figure, you **may** repeat **that** number as what they paid. Their number, not our guess. Do not invent an invoice they did not state.
2. **Not uplift.** Paying £2,000 does not mean the sale price rises by £2,000. Do not say “add £2,000 to the ask,” “buyers pay 40% of the invoice,” or “a wrap adds £800.”
3. **Gain = band.** Strong / Modest / Little / mixed / Negative from `value-gain.md` for that kind of work. Typical UK private-sale / part-exchange reaction, not a valuation. Modest means it helps the sale and **rarely covers the invoice**.
4. **`repair_cost` is the job, not the gain.** Call it only for a named published job (cambelt, clutch, MOT, and the other value-gain slugs). Say the headline as a planning range for the **work**. Gain stays a band even when the job has a headline.
5. **Vehicle card.** Record date, mileage, and the invoice they paid (spend) plus the **skill-stated band**. Do not invent a resale premium.

## Owner copy

≤120 words. Swap the band for the matching work in `value-gain.md`. Use their invoice figure only if they gave one; the £2,000 below is the live-rule example of spend, not a default.

```
[Value] You paid £2,000. That is the invoice — what you spent — not what the next buyer adds. Paying £2,000 does not mean the sale price rises by £2,000. We publish no gain in pounds. The gain is the band for this kind of work: Strong, Modest, Little / mixed, or Negative. Buyers rarely pay an invoice back pound-for-pound.

[Record] Put that invoice, the date, and the mileage on your vehicle card at obdcode.co.uk. The card stores your spend and this band, not a made-up premium.
```

No invoice yet: do not guess what they paid. Band only, then “record the invoice when you have it.”

## Red lines

1. No pound figure for **gain** unless it is their invoice, repeated as spend, not as uplift.
2. No invented invoice. No “buyers pay 40%.” No “adds £800.”
3. No `repair_cost` headline spoken as resale extra. Job range ≠ gain.
4. No how-to for wrap, remap, delete, weld, or lift. Price effect only.
5. No diagnosis of a lamp via a value band. No picker on a value-only question.
6. No real plate in the card speech or in git.
7. Safety / legality before any buyer-taste talk. Do not put this block above a Stop line.

## Pass versus fail

- Pass: “You paid £2,000. That is spend. On sale the gain is the band — not another £2,000.”
- Fail: “You paid £2,000, so the sale price rises by £2,000.”
- Pass: **[Value]** is Strong / Modest / Little / mixed / Negative for that work. Typical buyer reaction, not a valuation.
- Fail: “a wrap adds £800” / “buyers pay 40% of the invoice.”
- Pass: repeating **their** written invoice as what they paid, then the band.
- Fail: inventing an invoice they did not state, or treating that figure as uplift.
- Pass: `repair_cost` for a named published job as the **work** range; gain still the band.
- Fail: quoting a cambelt or clutch headline as pounds the next buyer will add.
- Pass: no invoice → band only; “we publish no gain in pounds.”
- Fail: filling the gap with a model guess or a made-up premium.
- Pass: **[Record]** their invoice, date, mileage, and this band on the vehicle card.
- Fail: printing the plate; uploading photos; inventing a resale premium in the card speech.
- Pass: lamp + invoice question → statement and outlook, then a separate **[Value]** that still uses this rule.
- Fail: value pounds above Stop / recovery; diagnosing the lamp from the band.
