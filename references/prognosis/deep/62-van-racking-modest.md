## Owner copy

≤120 words. Speak this, then stop. No how-to.

Neat, documented van racking or ply-lining is Modest on a working van. Trades pay for a ready van. Private buyers may not. It helps the sale; it rarely covers the invoice. We publish no gain in pounds. The bill you paid is spend, not an uplift. Messy or homemade kit is not this band. A documented cambelt or service history usually moves the bid more. Record the date, mileage, and that invoice on a vehicle card at obdcode.co.uk.

## Band

**Modest on a working van.** Typical UK private-sale / part-exchange reaction, not a valuation. Modest means it helps the sale and rarely covers the invoice.

| Work | Band | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Van racking / ply-lining (neat, documented) | Modest on a working van | Trades pay for a ready van; private buyers may not |

The qualifier is the **working van**. Trade buyers often pay for a ready load bay. Private buyers — empty-space, camper, or family use — may ignore it or want it out. That is still Modest on a working van, not Strong, and not a private-car premium.

Do **not** call this band when:

- The vehicle is a **private car**, not a working van
- The kit is messy, homemade, rusty, or unsafe — not neat, not documented
- They want install, weld, or payload how-to

Do not call racking Strong. Documented cambelt, wet belt, clutch, or full service history usually moves the bid more.

Do **not** call `repair_cost` for racking or ply. There is no published job slug and no modification-gain API. Gain stays this band.

## Record line

Point them to a vehicle card on [obdcode.co.uk](https://obdcode.co.uk). The skill does not build the card.

Record:

- What was done (neat, documented van racking and/or ply-lining — legal presentation only)
- Date and mileage
- Invoice total **they paid** (their number, repeated as spend)
- This band: **Modest on a working van**

Do not print the plate. Do not invent a resale premium.

## Red lines

1. No how-to for racking, ply, shelves, weld, or lift. Price effect only.
2. No invented gain in pounds. Never “adds £800” or “buyers pay 40% of the invoice.” There is no modification-gain API.
3. Their invoice is **spend**, not uplift. Paying £2,000 does not raise the sale price by £2,000.
4. No diagnosis of a lamp via this band.
5. No real plate in speech or in git. Illustrative speech: **your Transit** (or looked-up van). Worked plate in prose only `AB12CDE`.
6. Do not open the lamp picker on a racking question with no lamp.
7. Do not call `repair_cost` for racking or ply. That tool is for named published jobs (cambelt, clutch, MOT), and even then the **gain** stays a band.
8. Safety / legality before any “this might look nicer to a buyer.” Do not put this block above a Stop line.
9. Do not call messy, homemade, or private-car racking Modest.
