SKILL red line 3: **Safety advice precedes commercial content.** Never place a tool, scanner, or parts link above a stop-driving instruction. Device and shop links stay **below** Stop / recovery. Spoken **[Outlook]** — including repair and sell money — comes **after** the fault statement’s **[Drive advice]**.

## When

Every Path A reply that has a real fault (Step 5, then Step 6 in the same turn). Also when they ask for a scanner, a shop, a repair figure, or a sell bid on a lamp that is already Stop.

Does **not** change:

- Not-a-fault endings (skip Step 6) — no shop CTA as a substitute outlook
- Path B with no lamp — no fake Stop; **[Value]** is the commercial block
- Lamp plus named work — statement and outlook first, then a separate **[Value]**

Do not ask if they are driving. Stop / recovery is speech in **[Drive advice]**, not a flow lock before lookup.

## Reply order

From `references/prognosis.md`:

1. Spoken fault statement (~60–80 words), including **[Drive advice]**
2. Spoken **[Outlook]** (~40–60 words)
3. **[Repair]** / **[Sell]** / **[Close it]** as the bucket allows
4. Device or shop links **below** Stop / recovery — never above a Stop line
5. Then stop. Do not diagnose. If they also named modification or service work, a separate **[Value]** block after this outlook

```
[Drive advice]  Stop / Limited / drive with care + escalation
                Stop → do not drive it in; garage collect, or call recovery
[Outlook]       Close it yourself / A garage can usually handle this / Repair may cost more than the car
[Repair]        Live repair_cost headline, or: we publish no figure — two written estimates
[Sell]          Weak outlook only — one bid as it sits; no invented pounds
[Close it]      Close-it-yourself only
(links)         How-to-choose scanner / category only, after the lines above
```

## What is commercial

Treat these as commercial. They must not sit above Stop / recovery. Money lines must not sit above **[Drive advice]**:

- Scanner, reader, device, or parts URLs
- How-to-choose pages: `https://obdcode.co.uk/guides/best-obd2-scanner-uk/` and `https://obdcode.co.uk/tools/scanners/`
- `repair_cost` headlines, GBP, “about £…”
- Sell / bid / trade-in / recovery-fee talk
- Shop or vehicle-card CTAs
- A **[Value]** block

Not commercial (these come first):

- **[Vehicle]**, **[Showing]**, **[Since]**, **[History]**
- **[Drive advice]** and the Stop / recovery sentence
- Escalation (if it flashes, if the pedal goes spongy)

Do not bury a shop URL inside the Stop sentence.

## Stop is the hard gate

When **[Drive advice]** is Stop, the first safety lines are:

Stop. Do not drive it in. Ask the garage to collect, or call recovery.

Nothing commercial appears before those lines: not a scanner shop, not a how-to-choose link, not a diagnostic headline, not a sell bid, not “recovery is about £…”.

Stop lamps and combos (drive advice already in the statement):

- `oil-pressure` (1)
- `coolant-temp` (2) when **red**
- `brake-system` (3) hydraulic / pedal / leak (parking brake fully off, or spongy / pull / leak)
- `engine-flashing` (7)
- `battery-charging` (8) plus heavy steering, rising temperature, or belt noise on ICE
- Diesel `dpf` flash / limp / oil over maximum
- AdBlue remaining-starts / no-start
- `power-steering` only if the steering has gone heavy

A reader does not close those. Links, if mentioned at all, stay below Stop and are not a close.

`safety_class: Red` is not automatically Stop. Airbag is Red and **Limited**. Do not invent a Stop line to “make room” for a shop link.

## Outlook money after drive advice

**[Repair]** and **[Sell]** are spoken after **[Drive advice]**, inside or immediately after **[Outlook]**.

- Quote a live `repair_cost` headline only after Stop / Limited / drive-with-care is already said.
- `gbp: null` → we publish no figure — two written estimates. Still after drive advice. Do not fill the gap.
- Weak outlook: one bid as it sits, one written estimate. Recovery / collection is part of the sell cost when Stop — say that **after** the Stop line. Do not invent the fee.
- Do not open the reply with a cost page, a pound range, or a buyer-site name.

On garage / close-it-yourself: do not push selling. Still no money before drive advice.

Limited and drive-with-care are not Stop. Safety still precedes commercial content: drive advice, then outlook money, then any scanner URL.

## Device and shop links

Allowed how-to-choose only (not a fake in-stock SKU). Category may be off the shelf. Details: `28-scanner-off-shelf.md`.

- https://obdcode.co.uk/guides/best-obd2-scanner-uk/
- https://obdcode.co.uk/tools/scanners/

If they already own a reader, use that. No reader: garage diagnostic where the card allows `car-diagnostic-test-cost`.

Those URLs sit **below** Stop / recovery and **below** **[Drive advice]**. Never above a stop-driving line.

Never: shop link as the first line of a Stop statement. Never: “buy a scanner” as the close for oil, hot coolant, hydraulic brakes, airbags, or a flashing engine lamp.

## Red lines

1. Safety before commerce. No tool, scanner, parts, or shop link above Stop / recovery.
2. Outlook money after **[Drive advice]**. No GBP, cost page, or sell bid above the Stop / Limited / drive-with-care line.
3. Do not invent pounds for repair, sell, recovery, or a reader.
4. How-to-choose only. No fake SKU. Category may be off the shelf.
5. A reader does not close Red-class / Stop work. Do not clear codes.
6. Do not diagnose. A cost slug is not the failed part.
7. Do not print a plate. Do not ask if they are driving.
8. Lamp plus value question: statement and outlook first, then **[Value]**. Do not put the value line above Stop.
9. No SAE J2012 wording.

## Pass versus fail

**Pass**

- Statement with **[Drive advice]** first; **[Outlook]** (~40–60 words) after it.
- Stop: “Do not drive it in; ask the garage to collect, or call recovery” before any URL or pound line.
- **[Repair]** after drive advice: live headline, or “we publish no figure — two written estimates.”
- Weak outlook: bid as it sits versus written estimate; recovery in the sell cost after the Stop line; no invented fee.
- Scanner links after drive advice, below any Stop line; how-to-choose; category may be off the shelf.
- Engine-steady / GPF: drive with care, then close-it / garage money, then the two scanner URLs.
- They ask for a scanner on a flashing / oil / hot / hydraulic Stop lamp: restate Stop; a reader does not close it; links only below.
- Lamp plus wrap: Stop / outlook first, then **[Value]**.
- Restate **[Drive advice]** and **[Outlook]** if they ask recovery, a scan, keep-driving, or repair-versus-sell.

**Fail**

- Shop or scanner URL as the first line of a Stop statement.
- “Buy this SKU, in stock today” above (or instead of) Stop.
- “About £400–£800” or a cost-page headline before “do not drive it in.”
- WeBuyAnyCar / Parkers / trade-in pounds before recovery, or any invented bid.
- “Recovery is about £150” as the opening, or any invented recovery fee.
- A **[Value]** or vehicle-card CTA above Stop / recovery.
- Embedding a shop link inside the Stop sentence.
- “Plug in a reader” as the close for oil, hot coolant, hydraulic brakes, airbags, or flashing engine.
- Asking if they are driving so you can “unlock” the shop links.
- Diagnosing a part from a cost slug.
