# 55 · Glow 13 skip (preheat)

Not a second SKILL. Live rules: `SKILL.md`, `references/prognosis.md`, `references/prognosis-cards.md`. If this file disagrees, those win. Do not diagnose. Do not invent pounds. Do not print a plate. Do not add a 14th lamp id.

This note is the **not-a-fault split** for lamp **13** (`glow-plug`). Garage copy for the fault branch lives in `references/prognosis/deep/13-glow-plug.md`.

Diesel coil only. Amber coiled spiral. Petrol, hybrid, and electric boards ghost slot 13 — keep that board; this skip file does not run.

---

## When

After a valid 13, ask once if behaviour is unknown: **went out after start**, **stays on**, or **flashes**. That is classification, not a parts guess. Do not assume it went out. Do not ask if they are driving.

| Behaviour | Path |
|---|---|
| On with ignition, **then out after start** (a few seconds) | **Preheat.** Not a fault. **No garage card. No Step 6.** Stop after the short note. |
| Key-on coil that **went out** before they started | Same skip. Still preheat / bulb cycle, not a fault. |
| **Stays on** after the engine has started | Real fault. Bucket **`garage`**. Run Step 6 in the same turn as the statement. |
| **Flashes** while running | Real fault. Same **`garage`** bucket. Do **not** switch the id to 7. |

Do not skip because the car is old, new, high-mileage, or a van. Preheat is skip because the cycle finished, not because the owner is unworried.

If they also named wrap / service / value: still **skip Step 6**, then a separate **[Value]** block. A preheat cycle does not become a repair-or-sell outlook.

Vans: same skip. Say **your Transit**.

2. No garage card and no Step 6 on the skip. No diagnostic fee for a lamp that went out.
3. Do not name the failed part on either branch (“it’s the glow plugs”, “it’s the controller”).
4. Do not treat a flashing diesel coil as id **7**, and do not reuse petrol cat-fire copy.

## Pass / fail

**Pass**

- 13 on, then out after start → “That is the preheat cycle. Not a fault. No garage card. No Step 6.”
- Ask once: went out after start, stays on, or flashes — then take that branch.
- Stays on after start, or flashes while running → **`garage`**. “A garage can usually handle this.” Call `car-diagnostic-test-cost`. “The lamp does not name the part.”
- Flashing coil stays id **13**. Do not retarget 7.
- Ghost 13 on a petrol picture: keep the petrol board; this diesel skip does not run.
- Preheat plus a wrap question → skip outlook, then a separate **[Value]** only.
- Your 2016 Fiesta / your Transit. No plate.

**Fail**

- On-then-out → a garage-ready card, **[Ask the garage]**, **[Outlook]**, or Step 6.
- On-then-out → calling `car-diagnostic-test-cost` or any slug.
- On-then-out → “your plugs are failing” / “plugs are fine, that’s a pass.”
- Assuming it went out without asking when they only said “glow light.”
- “It’s the glow plugs, about £200 for a set of four.”
- Switching a flashing coil to engine-flashing (7) or “likely a catalytic converter.”
- DIY plug steps, a reader “to test glow plugs,” or a sell bid in pounds on the skip.
- Treating ghost 13 as diesel preheat, or shrinking the board toward electric.
- “Are you still driving?”
