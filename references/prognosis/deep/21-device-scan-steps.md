# 21 · Device scan steps (engine-steady / GPF)

Not a second SKILL. If this note disagrees with `SKILL.md`, `references/prognosis.md`, or `references/prognosis-cards.md`, those three win. Do not diagnose. Do not invent pounds. Do not add a 14th lamp id.

Allowed close-it-yourself sequence when the bucket is `device`: plug in, read **stored codes and freeze frame**, write them down, **do not clear**.

## When

Run these steps only when **both** are true: outlook is good (not `poor`), and the card is `device`.

- **`engine-steady` (lamp 6)** — amber engine outline, they have said it is **not** flashing. Fuel cap clicked tight is the only extra driveway check on this lamp.
- **Unmatched petrol / hybrid GPF** — exhaust-dots, or pick of 9 on a petrol board. Not diesel DPF. Scan the same way. No motorway-regen copy.

Do **not** use this sequence for red lamps or Red-class work. A reader does not close oil-pressure, red coolant-temp, hydraulic brake-system, airbag-srs, or flashing engine (7). It does not close charging Stop combos, DPF limp / oil-over-max, AdBlue remaining-starts, or EV turtle / HV text.

If they already own a reader, use that. If they need to choose one, link how-to-choose — not a fake in-stock SKU. The scanners category may be **off the shelf**.

- https://obdcode.co.uk/guides/best-obd2-scanner-uk/
- https://obdcode.co.uk/tools/scanners/

Those URLs sit **below** Stop / recovery, and below **[Drive advice]** in the spoken card. Never above a stop-driving line. Safety precedes commercial content even when these two paths are not Stop.

No reader, or they would rather not scan: garage path. Call `car-diagnostic-test-cost`. Say the tool headline, or that we publish no figure. Do not invent a diagnostic price here.

## Owner copy

Plug the reader in with the ignition in the handbook position. Keep the engine off unless the reader says otherwise. Read the stored codes and the freeze frame. Write both down for the garage. Do not clear the lamp. On a steady engine lamp only, click the fuel cap fully tight — that is the only extra driveway check. A scan is the next step; the lamp does not name the part. Use a reader you already own, or the how-to-choose pages — the shop may be off the shelf, not a named product in stock. No reader: a garage diagnostic test is the alternative. Scanner links come after drive advice, never above a stop instruction.

(114 words. Spoken **[Close it]** only. Do not add a parts guess, a SKU, or a pound figure.)

## Red lines

1. Not for red lamps. No driveway scan close for oil pressure, hot coolant, hydraulic brakes, airbags, or a flashing engine lamp.
2. Never advise clearing codes or the lamp as a fix.
3. Never present a scanner, SKU, or shop link as in stock. Category may be off the shelf. How-to-choose only.
4. Never place a tool, scanner, or parts link above Stop / recovery or above **[Drive advice]**.
5. Never invent GBP for a reader or a diagnostic. Use `repair_cost` in live chat; this file has no prices.
6. Never recite SAE J2012 wording. A code number is a fact to hand over, not a named failed part.
7. GPF is not DPF. No diesel regen copy. No scan-tool forced regen on any path.
8. Fuel cap is engine-steady only. It does not close GPF or any other lamp.
9. Do not treat freeze frame as optional. Stored codes **and** freeze frame, then write them down.

## Pass versus fail

- Pass: plug in (handbook ignition, engine off unless the reader says otherwise), stored codes **and** freeze frame, written down, not cleared.
- Fail: “clear it and see if it comes back.”
- Pass: how-to-choose links to `/guides/best-obd2-scanner-uk/` and `/tools/scanners/`; say the category may be off the shelf.
- Fail: “buy this SKU, in stock today.”
- Pass: scanner links after drive advice, below any Stop line.
- Fail: shop link as the first line of a Stop statement.
- Pass: “a scan is the next step; the lamp does not name the part.”
- Fail: naming lambda, cat, coil, or GPF soot from the code; any J2012 definition text.
- Pass: GPF — same read-and-write sequence, no motorway loop.
- Fail: GPF “do a regen like a diesel DPF.”
- Pass: flashing engine / oil / hot coolant / hydraulic brakes / airbag → no device close; restated Stop or garage.
- Fail: “plug in a reader” as the close for those lamps.
- Pass: no reader → garage diagnostic; published figure or “we publish no figure.”
- Fail: a guessed scan fee in pounds with no tool result.
