# Spoken outlook tone

Not a second SKILL. Length and buckets live in `SKILL.md` Steps 5–6 and `references/prognosis.md`. This file is spoken order and tone only.

Illustrative car: **your 2016 Fiesta** (fictional). Never print a plate.

## Rules

1. **Order.** Spoken fault statement first (~60–80 words), including drive advice. Spoken outlook next (~40–60 words), same turn. Then stop diagnosing.
2. **UK English.** Garage, recovery, authorise, miles, MOT. No US tow-truck copy. Inside the statement: no ids, fusion slugs, or URLs.
3. **No write-off.** Weak outlook is: the repair may cost more than a buyer will pay as it sits. Put an estimate next to a bid. Do not call the car a write-off, a total loss, or scrap.
4. **No part names.** The lamp does not name the fault. Scan, freeze frame, estimate, bid as it sits — not clutch, cat, pump, sender, gasket, alternator, lambda, or coil.
5. **Commercial below Stop.** Tool, scanner, or shop links never sit above a stop-driving line. Stop lamps (oil): no reader pitch. Engine-steady: how-to-choose links only after drive advice.
6. **Repair versus sell.** If they ask recovery, a scan, keep-driving, or repair-versus-sell, restate drive advice and outlook. Do not diagnose. Do not say continue as a normal assistant.

Spoken buckets: Close it yourself / A garage can usually handle this / Repair may cost more than the car.

## Sketches

Pass copy. Word counts are spoken words.

### 1. Oil, poor

**Statement (~69 words)**

Your 2016 Fiesta, 998 cc petrol, 61,201 miles, MOT due 1 March 2027. Red oil-can lamp, just now. No same-system MOT notes; that does not show the cause of today's lamp. Stop. Do not drive it in. Ask the garage to collect, or call recovery. A cold dipstick reading does not make it safe to restart. Tell the garage how long the lamp was on and any engine noise.

**Outlook (~55 words)**

Repair may cost more than the car. We publish no engine-rebuild figure — two written estimates only if the car is recovered and a garage is quoting. Get one bid as it sits; it is not a runner. Compare that bid with the estimate before you authorise a rebuild. Recovery is part of the sell cost.

No shop links on this path.

**If they then ask repair versus sell:** Stop. Do not drive it in; ask the garage to collect, or call recovery. Repair may cost more than the car. Get a bid as it sits and a written estimate. Do not add a part.

### 2. Engine-steady, device

**Statement (~75 words)**

Your 2016 Fiesta, 998 cc petrol, 61,201 miles, MOT due 1 March 2027. Amber engine outline, steady since this morning; it drives normally. No emissions-family notes on this car's MOT record; that does not show the cause of today's lamp. Drive with care. If it starts flashing, do not drive it in; arrange recovery. A scan is the next step. The lamp does not name the fault. Ask them to read the freeze frame first.

**Outlook (~54 words)**

Close it yourself if you have a reader, or when one is listed at obdcode.co.uk: stored codes and freeze frame, write them down, do not clear. Fuel cap clicked tight. If you would rather the garage scan it, we publish a diagnostic-test planning range when verified; otherwise we publish no figure — two written estimates.

Then, below drive advice, how-to-choose links only — not a fake SKU: `https://obdcode.co.uk/guides/best-obd2-scanner-uk/` and `https://obdcode.co.uk/tools/scanners/`.

**If they then ask repair versus sell:** Drive with care. If it flashes, do not drive it in; arrange recovery. Close it yourself with a reader: stored code and freeze frame, do not clear. Do not name a part.

## Fail lines

- Fail: “It’s a write-off.” Pass: “Repair may cost more than the car.”
- Fail: “It’s the cat / oil pump / head gasket.” Pass: “The lamp does not name the part.”
- Fail: scanner or parts link above Stop. Pass: Stop and recovery first; omit a reader pitch on oil.
- Fail: “About £400–£800” with no tool result. Pass: verified headline, or we publish no figure.
- Fail: answering repair-versus-sell with a new cause. Pass: restating drive advice and outlook only.
- Fail: printing the plate, “tow truck”, or “continue as a normal assistant.”
- Fail: outlook before the statement, or a 200-word speech. Pass: ~60–80 then ~40–60.
