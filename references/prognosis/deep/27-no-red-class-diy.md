# 27 · No Red-class driveway how-to

Not a second SKILL. If this note disagrees with `SKILL.md`, `references/prognosis.md`, or `references/prognosis-cards.md`, those win. Do not diagnose. Do not invent pounds. Do not add a 14th lamp id.

**Close it yourself never covers Red-class work.** Oil, hot coolant, hydraulic brakes, airbags, and a flashing engine lamp are not driveway jobs. A reader does not fix them. Outlook **may** be sell. There is no `[Close it]` block.

`safety_class: Red` is not Stop. Airbag is Red and **Limited**. The other four in this list are **Stop** once they are a real fault.

## Information, not a close

These glances do **not** become how-to and do **not** make the car safe to restart or drive in:

- Oil: dipstick once **completely cold**. Correct level does not clear the lamp.
- Hot coolant: tank and floor once **cold**. Never open a hot cap.
- Hydraulic brakes: level **through** the translucent reservoir, cap on. Topping up is not a repair. No bleeding, pads, or lifting.
- Airbag: nothing owner-safe. No clock-spring, seat, or scan-clear DIY. Pyrotechnic parts stay at a workshop.
- Flashing engine: nothing. Do not keep driving to see. Do not run the steady-engine fuel-cap close.

A small OBD device does not change the next step on any of these five. Do not describe freeze-frame steps as a repair. Do not force a DPF regen with a scan tool (that ban is general; it is not a close here either).

## Sell (when the bucket is `poor`)

There is no sell-price tool. Do not invent Parkers / WeBuyAnyCar / trade-in pounds. Do not say write-off.

1. Repair: verified `repair_cost` headline only if that lamp’s allowlist says to call a slug; otherwise **we publish no figure** — two written estimates. Empty allowlist (oil; hydraulic brakes as cause) is an answer, not a gap.
2. Sell: get **one** bid **as the car sits** (not a runner when Stop). Get **one** written estimate.
3. If the estimate is larger than the bid, selling is often the better outlook.
4. Recovery / collection is part of the sell cost when **[Drive advice]** is Stop. Do not invent that fee.

Airbag default is garage: do **not** push selling. If they will not repair SRS, say a lamp-on airbag car is worth less and may not be legal to sell as a safe runner — still no invented bid.

## Red lines

1. No repair steps for Red-class work: no airbag / pretensioner / SRS wiring, no brake hydraulics or bleeding, no high-pressure fuel, nothing that needs the car lifted. Refer out. Say why (reader does not fix it; outlook may be sell).
2. Close-it-yourself never covers oil, hot coolant, hydraulic brakes, airbags, or a flashing engine lamp.
3. Never advise clearing a lamp or codes as a fix.
4. Never invent GBP for repair, sell, recovery, or “typical rebuild.” `gbp: null` is the answer.
5. Never place a tool, scanner, or parts link above Stop / recovery.
6. Do not treat Red as Stop on airbag. Do not treat airbag garage as a driveway scan close.
7. Do not diagnose: not the pump, sender, bearing, head gasket, master cylinder, clock spring, coil, cylinder, or cat.
8. No SAE J2012 wording. No real registration in this file or in speech.
9. Do not instruct wrap, remap, or filter delete. Safety outlook first if a lamp is also lit.
10. Cold dipstick / cold tank / reservoir glance is information, not permission to drive.

- Pass: no `[Close it]` on these five. “A reader does not fix this.”

- Pass: oil / red coolant / hydraulic brakes / flashing engine → Stop; do not drive it in; recover.
- Fail: “drive it slowly to the garage” / “top it up and go” / “restart and watch the gauge.”
- Pass: airbag → Limited, garage, no DIY. Direct there, no extra journeys.
- Fail: airbag Stop, or airbag clock-spring / scan-clear how-to.
- Pass: `poor` lamps — repair may cost more than the car; one bid as it sits vs one written estimate; recovery in sell cost when Stop.
- Fail: invented trade-in or repair pounds; “it’s a write-off”; pushing sell on airbag garage.
- Pass: “we publish no figure” when the allowlist is empty or `gbp: null`.
