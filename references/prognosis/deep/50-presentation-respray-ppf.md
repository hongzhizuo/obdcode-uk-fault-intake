# Presentation: respray, PPF, stereo, exhaust, towbar, van racking

Not a second SKILL. Live rules: `references/value-gain.md`, SKILL Step 7. Sale-price effect only. No how-to.

Not this card: colour wrap / wheels / lowering (`64-wrap-wheels-value.md`); documented service / belt / clutch (`54-maintenance-strong-value.md`); illegal exhaust, delete, remap (`71-illegal-mods-negative.md`). Interior refresh / aftermarket seats stay **Little / mixed** on the live table — do not invent a how-to here.

## When

They name a **professional respray**, **PPF**, **ceramic**, **stereo / screens**, **legal like-for-like exhaust**, **towbar**, or **van racking / ply-lining**, or they ask whether that work **adds value**.

- Path B: no lamp → Step 7 only. Lookup the plate if they gave one, for year / make / model in the value line. **No** dashboard picker.
- Lamp also lit: fault statement + repair-or-sell outlook **first**, then this **[Value]** block. Paint, film, or a stereo does not close a lamp.
- Speak the **matching** band. Do not dump the whole table.

## Owner copy

≤120 words. Price effect only. Repeat **their** invoice as spend if they already named it. Never invent uplift pounds.

**Professional respray — Modest**

```
[Value] Modest. A professional respray of damaged panels tidies the asking price. It rarely covers the invoice. A cheap respray can look worse than the scuff. We publish no gain in pounds. The bill you paid is spend, not an uplift.
```

**PPF / ceramic — Modest / mixed**

```
[Value] Modest / mixed. Quality PPF or ceramic on a late car helps a careful buyer. Many will not pay it back. We publish no gain in pounds.
```

**Stereo / screens — Little**

```
[Value] Little. Stereo and screens do not come back as the invoice. You will not get that spend back.
```

**Legal like-for-like exhaust — Little**

```
[Value] Little. A quiet, legal, like-for-like exhaust can help. A roar usually does not. Illegal or off-map exhaust is Negative, not this card.
```

**Towbar — Modest on the right car**

```
[Value] Modest on the right car. Type-approved and documented: towing buyers pay; others ignore it. It rarely covers the invoice.
```

**Van racking / ply-lining — Modest on a working van**

```
[Value] Modest on a working van. Neat, documented racking or ply-lining: trades pay for a ready van; private buyers may not.
```

## Band

Typical UK private-sale / part-exchange reaction, not a valuation. **Modest** helps the sale and rarely covers the invoice. **Little / mixed** is taste.

| Work | Band | Skill line |
|---|---|---|
| Professional respray of damaged panels | Modest | Tidies the asking price; a cheap respray can look worse than the scuff |
| Quality PPF / ceramic on a late car | Modest / mixed | Helps a careful buyer; many will not pay it back |
| Legal like-for-like exhaust | Little | Quiet quality can help; a roar usually does not |
| Stereo / screens | Little | You will not get the invoice back |
| Towbar (type-approved, documented) | Modest on the right car | Towing buyers pay; others ignore it |
| Van racking / ply-lining (neat, documented) | Modest on a working van | Trades pay for a ready van; private buyers may not |

A cheap or poor respray is **not** Modest. Say it can look worse than the scuff. Do not call it Strong. Documented cambelt or service history usually moves the bid more.

Do **not** call `repair_cost` for paint, PPF, ceramic, stereo, exhaust, towbar, or racking. There is no published job slug and no modification-gain API. Gain stays a band. If they already have a written invoice, repeat **that** figure as what they paid, then the band. Paying £2,000 does not raise the sale price by £2,000.

## Red lines

1. No how-to for respray, PPF, ceramic, stereo, exhaust, towbar, or racking. Price effect only.
2. No invented gain pounds. Never “adds £800” or “buyers pay 40% of the invoice.”
3. Their invoice is **spend**, not uplift.
4. Cheap respray is not Modest. Do not coach a DIY paint or film job.
5. Legal exhaust stays **Little**. Illegal / off-map exhaust is **Negative** — do not instruct; that is `71-illegal-mods-negative.md`.
6. Towbar speech is type-approved and documented. Do not install steps.
7. No diagnosis of a lamp via this band. No picker on a value-only question.
8. No real plate in speech or in git.
9. Safety / legality before any “this might look nicer to a buyer.” Do not put this block above a Stop line.
