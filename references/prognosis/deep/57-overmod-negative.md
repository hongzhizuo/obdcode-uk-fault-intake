# Over-modded show car — Negative for most buyers

Not a second SKILL. Live rules: `SKILL.md`, `references/value-gain.md`. This file only fills **Step 7** sale-price speech for a stacked show-car build. No how-to.

One wrap or one set of aftermarket wheels is **Little / mixed** (`64-wrap-wheels-value.md`). Illegal delete, unapproved remap, or off-map exhaust is **Negative** as a defect (`71-illegal-mods-negative.md`). This card is the **whole show car**: widebody, air, camber, interior, wrap, and wheels stacked as a build. Band stays **Negative for most buyers**.

## When

- Path B: no lamp, and they name a show car, over-modded build, stance / air-ride / widebody package, or “I’ve just had the full build done” — or they ask whether that work **adds value**, including on part-exchange.
- Lookup the plate if they gave one, for year / make / model in the value line. **No** dashboard picker.
- Lamp also lit: fault statement + repair-or-sell outlook **first**, then this **[Value]** block. A show build does not close a lamp.
- Not this file: a single legal wrap or wheel set (Little / mixed); documented cambelt / service history (Strong); matching tyres / MOT (Modest); DPF / GPF / cat delete or emissions cheat (Negative, legal / test problem).

## Owner copy

≤120 words. Price effect only.

```
[Value] Negative for most buyers. A heavily modified show car shrinks the buyer pool. A small specialist group may still want that look. Ordinary private buyers and part-exchange desks usually bid less than they would for a comparable stock car. We publish no gain in pounds. The build you paid for is spend, not an uplift. A documented service history or a dated cambelt invoice is what usually moves a mainstream bid.

[Record] If the work is legal, put the date, mileage, and that invoice on your vehicle card at obdcode.co.uk.
```

(71 words in the Value paragraph.)

If they already named a written invoice, you may repeat **that** figure as what they paid, then this band. Paying £2,000 does not mean the sale price rises by £2,000.

Do not present the show build as a premium. The vehicle card is for service, repair, presentation, and **legal** modification. If the same car also has a delete or an illegal exhaust, that work is not a selling point (`71-illegal-mods-negative.md`).

## Band

**Negative for most buyers.** Shrinks the mainstream pool or the bid. A small specialist pool may still bid. Part-exchange is usually worse than a stock equivalent. Typical UK private-sale / part-exchange reaction, not a valuation.

| Work | Band | Skill line |
|---|---|---|
| Over-modded show car | Negative for most buyers | A small specialist pool; part-exchange usually worse |

A specialist who wants that exact look does **not** move the band to Modest, Little / mixed, or Strong. Dealers still have to retail or unwind the car; the PX desk usually bids less. Do not invent the discount in pounds.

There is **no** modification-gain API. Do not call `repair_cost` for a show build, wrap, kit, or air-ride. That tool is only for a **named published job** (cambelt, clutch, MOT) they already asked about as **work**. Gain stays this band even when a job has a headline.

## Red lines

1. No how-to: no wrap, kit, air-ride, camber, weld, lift, or “put it back to stock” steps. Price effect only.
2. No invented gain pounds. No “adds £800.” No invented PX discount. Their invoice is spend, not uplift.
3. Do not call the show build an upgrade for most buyers. A specialist pool is not a Strong band.
4. No diagnosis of a lamp via this band. No picker on a value-only question.
5. No real plate in speech or in git.
6. Safety / legality before any buyer-taste talk. Do not put this block above a Stop line.
7. Illegal work on the same car is a defect, not “specialist taste.” Do not instruct. Do not dump a legal show car into delete speech.
8. Do not stretch Strong maintenance (service history, dated belt) into a Strong show-car band. State the two effects separately if they named both.

## Pass / fail

- Pass: **[Value] Negative for most buyers.** Small specialist pool. Part-exchange usually worse. We publish no gain in pounds.
- Fail: “show cars always add value” / “buyers pay the build back” / “PX will love it.”
- Pass: their invoice, if they gave one, spoken as **spend**, then this band.
- Fail: invented uplift, or treating the invoice as a sale-price rise.
- Pass: no cluster picture on a value-only question.
- Fail: wrap / kit / air-ride / camber how-to, or “here is how to unwind it.”
- Pass: lamp + this question → statement and outlook, then a separate **[Value]**.
- Fail: value line above Stop / recovery; diagnosing the lamp from the band; printing the plate.
- Pass: one legal wrap or wheel set stays **Little / mixed** on its own card.
- Fail: calling a stacked show build Little / mixed or Strong, or calling a single wrap Negative-for-most.
- Pass: a delete or illegal exhaust on the same car stays **Negative** as a defect, not taste.
- Fail: “the specialist pool will pay extra for a delete,” or calling a legal show car illegal with no such work named.
- Pass: no `repair_cost` for the show build; a named cambelt slug is the **job**, not the gain.
- Fail: a guessed PX or resale figure in pounds.
