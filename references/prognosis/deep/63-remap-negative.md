# Unapproved remap / emissions cheat (Negative)

Not a second SKILL. Live rules: `SKILL.md`, `references/value-gain.md`. This file only fills **Step 7** sale-price speech for an unapproved remap or an emissions cheat. No how-to.

DPF / GPF / cat delete and illegal / off-map exhaust are **Negative** on `71-illegal-mods-negative.md`. A stacked show car is **Negative for most buyers** on `57-overmod-negative.md`. This card is the **software / cheat** row. Band stays **Negative**.

## When

- Path B: no lamp, and they name an unapproved remap, stage map, ECU flash, or emissions cheat — or ask whether that work **adds value**.
- A bare “remap” or “I’ve had it mapped” is this card. Do not ask how it was done.
- Lookup the plate if they gave one, for year / make / model in the value line. **No** dashboard picker.
- Lamp also lit: fault statement + repair-or-sell outlook **first**, then this **[Value]** block. A remap does not close a lamp. Do not suggest a map as a cheap DPF, EGR, AdBlue, or engine-lamp repair.
- Not this file: DPF / GPF / cat removal; illegal noise / off-map exhaust; documented cambelt / service history (Strong); a single legal wrap or wheel set (Little / mixed); a dealer or manufacturer software update they named as a recall or factory campaign.

## Owner copy

≤120 words. Price effect only.

```
[Value] Negative. An unapproved remap or an emissions cheat does not raise the sale price. It shrinks the buyer pool. The car can fail the MOT. Buyers, testers, and insurers treat it as a defect, not an upgrade. We publish no gain in pounds. Do not do this to a road car.

If that work is already on the car, the band is still Negative. Documented service or a cambelt invoice is what usually moves a bid.
```

No **[Record]** as a selling point. The vehicle card is for service, repair, presentation, and **legal** modification. Do not present this work as a premium.

## Band

**Negative.** Shrinks the buyer pool or the bid. Can fail the test. Illegal or MOT-hostile work is also a legal / test / insurer problem, not a valuation uplift.

| Work | Band | Skill line |
|---|---|---|
| Unapproved remap / emissions cheat | Negative | Shrinks the pool; can fail the test; do not instruct |

If they already have a written invoice, you may repeat **that** figure as what they paid, then this band. Spend is not uplift.

There is **no** modification-gain API. Do not call `repair_cost` for a remap or a cheat. That tool is only for a **named published job** (cambelt, clutch, MOT) they already asked about as **work**. Gain stays this band.

## Red lines

1. No how-to: no flash, map, blank, disable, hide-for-test, or “put it back” steps. Garage restoration is not this card.
2. No invented gain pounds. No “adds £800.” `repair_cost` is never a **gain**.
3. Do not call it an upgrade. Do not instruct.
4. Not legal advice beyond “do not do this to a road car.” No invented fines.
5. No diagnosis of a lamp via this band. No picker on a value-only question.
6. No real plate in speech or in git.
7. Safety / legality before any buyer-taste talk. Do not put this block above a Stop line.
8. MOT: testers may treat an emissions cheat or an unapproved map as a defect. Do not copy lamp speech “Expect a fail.”
9. Do not dump a DPF delete or an illegal exhaust into this remap paragraph — those stay on `71-illegal-mods-negative.md`. Do not dump a dealer recall flash into Negative.

## Pass / fail

- Pass: **[Value] Negative.** Shrinks the pool. Can fail the test. Do not instruct.
- Fail: “it adds power so it is worth more” / “buyers pay extra for a stage map.”
- Pass: “we publish no gain in pounds.”
- Fail: invented uplift, or treating their invoice as sale-price rise.
- Pass: no cluster picture on a value-only question.
- Fail: remap / flash / cheat how-to, or “map it to clear the lamp.”
- Pass: lamp + this question → statement and outlook, then a separate **[Value]**.
- Fail: value line above Stop / recovery; diagnosing the lamp from the band; printing the plate.
- Pass: DPF / GPF / cat delete stays on the delete card; illegal exhaust stays on the exhaust card.
- Fail: teaching a delete or a map as a DPF / engine-lamp fix.
