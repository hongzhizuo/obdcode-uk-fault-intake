## Owner copy

≤120 words. Labels are for the agent. UK English: garage, recovery, authorise, miles, MOT. No ids, fusion slugs, URLs, plates, or guessed GBP.

### Recovery (Stop already given)

Stop. Do not drive it in. Ask the garage to collect, or call recovery. Repair may cost more than the car. We publish no figure. Get one bid as it sits — it is not a runner — and one written estimate. Recovery is part of the sell cost. Do not invent that fee. The lamp does not name the part.

### Scan (engine-steady `device` already given)

Drive with care. If it starts flashing, do not drive it in; arrange recovery. A scan is the next step. The lamp does not name the fault. Close it yourself with a reader you already own, or when one is listed: stored codes and freeze frame, write them down, do not clear. Fuel cap clicked tight. No reader: a garage diagnostic test. We publish a planning headline when verified; otherwise we publish no figure — two written estimates.

### Keep-driving (do not soften Stop)

Stop. Do not drive it in. A slow run to the garage is still driving it in. Ask them to collect, or call recovery. Outlook is unchanged: repair may cost more than the car. Bid as it sits versus a written estimate. A cold dipstick is information, not permission to restart.

### Keep-driving (Limited — airbag / direct-there)

Limited. You may drive directly to the garage. No extra journeys. A garage can usually handle this. We publish no SRS figure — two written estimates. Do not recover it by default. Do not leave it until next year. This skill does not diagnose. The lamp does not name the part.

### Repair versus sell (weak)

Stop if that was the drive advice; otherwise keep the grade you already gave. Repair may cost more than the car. We publish no used-car price. Get one bid as it sits and one written estimate. Compare them before you authorise the job. Recovery is part of the sell cost when you must not drive it in. Do not call it a write-off.

### Repair versus sell (garage or close-it-yourself)

Keep the drive advice you already gave. A garage can usually handle this — or close it yourself where that bucket already applied. Do not push selling. The lamp still does not name the part. This skill does not diagnose.

### What is wrong (not a restatement trigger on its own)

This skill does not diagnose. The lamp does not name the part. Hand the statement to the garage. If you also need keep-driving or repair versus sell: use the matching sketch above. Do not say continue as a normal assistant.

## Do not change on restatement
