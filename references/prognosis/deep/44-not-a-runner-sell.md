# Stop lamps: bid as it sits

Oil-pressure, red coolant, flashing engine, hydraulic brake (parking brake off, or spongy pedal / pull / leak): **[Drive advice]** is Stop. Bucket `poor`. Usually not a runner; needs collection.

Sell: no published used-car price. One bid as it sits, one written estimate. Recovery is part of the sell cost. Do not invent that fee or pounds.

Do not tell them to drive it to a buyer. Do not say write-off.

Pass versus fail:

- Pass: “Get a bid as it sits. Buyer collects, or count recovery in the sell cost.”
- Fail: “Drive it to WeBuyAnyCar.”
- Fail: “Recovery is about £150.”
- Fail: “Typical trade-in is £800.”
