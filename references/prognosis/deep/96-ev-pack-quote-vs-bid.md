## Owner copy

Spoken **[Outlook]** after the statement. ≤120 words. No ids, no fusion slugs, no URLs, no plates, no invented GBP.

### Garage — no pack quote

A garage can usually handle this. We publish no figure for this class of high-voltage work. Ask two workshops for a written estimate. The lamp or message does not name the part. Drive directly there, no extra journeys, unless a red lamp or a stop lamp is also on — then do not drive it in; arrange recovery. This is not a driveway job. Do not treat it as the 12-volt rectangle or the skid-lines mark.

(80 words.)

### Poor — they already have a traction-pack quote

Repair may cost more than the car. We publish no pack price and no used-car price. Compare the traction-pack quote you already have with one bid as the car sits. If that quote is larger than the bid, selling is often the better outlook. Do not authorise the job until you have both numbers. Recovery is part of the sell cost if you must not drive it in.

(76 words.)

You may **repeat their quoted figure** as what they were already given. Do not replace it with a typical pack price. Do not add a second invented pack number.
