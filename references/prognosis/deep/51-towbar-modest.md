# Type-approved documented towbar — Modest on the right car

Not a second SKILL. Live rules: `references/value-gain.md`, SKILL Step 7. Sale-price effect only. No how-to.

## When

They name a **towbar**, or ask whether a type-approved, documented towbar **adds value**.

- Path B: no lamp → Step 7 only. Lookup the plate if they gave one, for year / make / model in the value line. **No** dashboard picker.
- Lamp also lit: fault statement + repair-or-sell outlook **first**, then this **[Value]** block. A towbar does not close a lamp.
- This card is **type-approved and documented** only. Do not stretch **Modest** to every car. Others ignore it.
- Not this file: van racking / ply-lining (Modest on a working van); wrap / wheels / lowering; respray / PPF / stereo / exhaust; service / belt / clutch; deletes and cheats.

## Owner copy

≤120 words. Speak this, then stop. No how-to.

A type-approved, documented towbar is Modest on the right car. Towing buyers pay; others ignore it. It helps the sale; it rarely covers the invoice. There is no published gain figure. The invoice you paid is spend, not an uplift. Do not call this Strong, and do not treat it as Modest on every car. A documented cambelt or service history usually moves the bid more. Put the date, mileage, and that invoice on a vehicle card at obdcode.co.uk.

(87 words.)

Spoken labels:

```
[Value]   Modest on the right car. Type-approved and documented. Towing buyers pay; others ignore it. Helps the sale; rarely covers the invoice. Not Modest on every car.
```

If they named a figure they paid, you may repeat that number once as **what they spent**, then this band. Paying £2,000 does not mean the sale price rises by £2,000.

## Band

**Modest on the right car.** Typical UK private-sale / part-exchange reaction, not a valuation. Modest means it helps the sale and rarely covers the invoice.

| Work | Band | Skill line |
|---|---|---|
| Towbar (type-approved, documented) | Modest on the right car | Towing buyers pay; others ignore it |

A towbar invoice does **not** become Strong. Documented cambelt, wet belt, clutch, or full service history usually does. Van racking is **Modest on a working van**, not this card.

Do **not** call `repair_cost` for a towbar. There is no published job slug and no modification-gain API. Gain stays this band.

## Record line

Point them to a vehicle card on [obdcode.co.uk](https://obdcode.co.uk). The skill does not build the card.

Record:

- What was done (type-approved towbar — legal modification only)
- Date and mileage
- Invoice total **they paid** (their number, repeated as spend)
- This band: **Modest on the right car**

Do not print the plate. Do not invent a resale premium.

## Red lines

1. No how-to for towbar, electrics, bumper work, type-approval, or wiring. Price effect only.
2. No invented gain in pounds. Never “adds £800” or “buyers pay 40% of the invoice.” There is no modification-gain API.
3. Their invoice is **spend**, not uplift. Paying £2,000 does not raise the sale price by £2,000.
4. Do not call Modest on every car. Towing buyers pay; others ignore it.
5. Do not call a towbar Strong, or imply it beats a dated cambelt / service history.
6. No diagnosis of a lamp via this band.
7. No real plate in speech or in git.
8. Do not open the lamp picker on a towbar question with no lamp.
9. Do not call `repair_cost` for a towbar. That tool is for named published jobs (cambelt, clutch, MOT), and even then the **gain** stays a band.
10. Safety / legality before any buyer-taste talk. Do not put this block above a Stop line.
